Enter quotes for the same products, grade and quantities in two markets. This tool calculates locally on your device.

NOT LIVE · যাচাইবিহীন তথ্য

These prices are typed by you, not verified by BazarTulona or any government service. No current market price, availability or savings claim is made. Check the official report and the seller before buying.

01 · তারিখ / Dates

When did you check each market?

Both quotes must be from the same day and no more than seven days old. Your device's date is used for the freshness check.

Do not compare a retail quote with a wholesale quote. Choose the basis actually used by each seller; this site cannot verify it.

02 · পণ্য / Products

Same grade, same unit, same quantity

Use one quantity for both markets. For each selected product, check the grade, unit and pack size with both sellers, then tick the confirmation. For unused products, leave quantity at zero and prices blank. Unit prices are in BDT (৳).

Nothing is uploaded, stored, shared or logged by this calculator. Closing or reloading the page clears your entries.

03 · ফলাফল / Result

Your comparison

Enable JavaScript and enter two dated price quotes to compare.

Verify before shopping

Department of Agricultural Marketing — official daily report (navigation only; no prices are imported here).

Check the observation date, actual shop, product grade, quantity, unit, retail basis and extra costs. A lower entered total is not a guarantee of the cheapest available shop.
